The map

What drives the prediction

  • Recent history (lag1): 23% — last month's reports predict this month's
  • Transit routes & stops: ~28% — busy areas report more
  • Season & month: ~22% — potholes spike after winter freeze-thaw
  • Trees & weather: ~16% — the rest

How it works

  • Pulls live Boston 311, weather (Open-Meteo), and MBTA transit data.
  • An XGBoost model scores each neighborhood for the current month.
  • Two modes: all 311 requests, or potholes only.
